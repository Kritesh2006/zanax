Flicker Room

Rhythmic light on closed eyes, paced like a guided session. Sit in a fully dark room, close your eyes, and hold the light 5–15 cm from your face.

Light source

Session

Length

8 min

Pulse tone

35%

Photosensitivity warning. Flashing at 3–30 Hz can trigger seizures, including in people with no history of epilepsy. Don't use this if you or close family have epilepsy, if you're under 18, or while alone the first time. Stop at once if you feel dizzy, nauseous or unwell.

During a session

  1. The first 20 seconds fade in gently.
  2. Tap anywhere (or press Esc) to stop instantly.
  3. The readout at the bottom shows the target rate and the rate your light is actually reaching.
