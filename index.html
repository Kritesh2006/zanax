Photosensitivity warning. Flashing at 3–30 Hz can trigger seizures, including in people with no history of epilepsy. Don't use flicker if you or close family have epilepsy, if you're under 18, or alone the first time. Keep eyes closed unless a session says otherwise. Stop if you feel dizzy, nauseous, or your eyes hurt.

What the presets are based on

  • Frequency and rhythmicity of flicker (FU Berlin): 10 Hz rhythmic strongest; irregular flicker much weaker.
  • 3 Hz vs 10 Hz flicker phenomena: vivid simple visuals, strongest at 10 Hz.
  • Ganzflicker vs Ganzfeld and Dreamachine-team comparison: flicker gives tunnels and spirals early, steady light gives slower, cloudier imagery.
  • Fechner colors: flickering a single color can make you see other colors, depending on frequency.
